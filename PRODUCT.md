# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Competition presenters and reviewers use Aegis Runtime during a short live demonstration. They need to follow a risky tool request from TaskGraph dependency through Runtime decision, approval, execution, and recovery without changing pages.

## Product Purpose

Aegis Runtime demonstrates that tool-enabled agents remain inside a risk-adaptive safety boundary. A successful demo makes the Runtime's decisions, dependencies, approvals, effects, and audit facts inspectable in one coherent flow.

## Brand Personality

Credible, precise, calm.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a marketing dashboard, a decorative workflow mockup, or an interface that hides safety-critical actions behind navigation. Do not use fabricated client-side Runtime state.

## Design Principles

- Keep the evidence and the action in the same visual context.
- Make dependency and blocking relationships legible before secondary metadata.
- Use Runtime-owned facts as the source of every visible safety state.
- Prefer short, deliberate presenter flows over feature density.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus indicators, and no required motion.
